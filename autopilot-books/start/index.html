Step 1: Check the fit

Autopilot Books is built for simple businesses, which is how it stays at $149. Your answers stay in your browser. Nothing is sent to me.

Do you have employees, or run payroll, including paying yourself through payroll?

Payroll isn't part of Autopilot Books. If it's just you on payroll in an S-corp, my S-Corp Owner plan at $399 a month fits. With employees, Essentials at $500 a month does. Book a 15-minute call and I'll set it up.

Do you collect sales tax?

Sales tax filings aren't part of Autopilot Books. Book a 15-minute call and I'll point you to the plan that covers them.

Is it one business, with one bank account and one credit card, and about 75 or fewer transactions a month?

You're past what Autopilot Books covers, so another plan fits better. See all plans, or book a 15-minute call and I'll match you to one.

Do you use QuickBooks Online, or are you willing to sign up for it?

Autopilot Books runs in your own QuickBooks Online, which is what lets most of the work happen automatically. Book a 15-minute call if you'd like to talk through the options.

Are your books current through last month, or is this a new business?
