Is it a fit?

Short answer: Autopilot Books fits if you have no employees, don't collect sales tax, run about 40 or fewer transactions a month through one bank account and one card, and are happy to handle questions by email.

A good fit

  • No payroll and no sales tax
  • Up to about 40 transactions a month

What you get every month

  • Bank and card feed rules that categorize your transactions automatically

Common questions

Is this just bookkeeping software?

No. Software does the sorting, and I review your books every month before anything reaches you. You get a person checking the numbers at a price close to software.

What if I go over 40 transactions?

A busy month is fine. If you're over the limits two months running, I'll talk with you about moving to Launch or Essentials before anything changes.

Can I call you?

Autopilot Books is email only, with replies within 2 business days. That's how the price stays at $149. If you want calls, Launch at $250 a month adds them, along with QuickBooks setup help and 1099s for up to 5 contractors.

Can you do my tax return too?

Yes. Autopilot clients get my plan-client price: $149 for a personal return, or $249 with a Schedule C. Or I'll send a clean year-end package to whoever prepares it now. Tax preparation.

I already have a bookkeeper. Is there something for me?

Yes. If you want to keep them, my Owner Dashboard & Cash Review is $125 a month: a monthly dashboard and review that sits on top of their work. Book a call and I'll set it up.

Is my information secure?

I work in your QuickBooks Online through accountant access, so you never share your login, and I never ask for passwords or full account numbers by email. You can remove my access at any time.

Are you a CPA?

No. Nova Heritage Group is an accounting and tax firm, not a CPA firm, and it doesn't perform audits, reviews, compilations or other attest services.
