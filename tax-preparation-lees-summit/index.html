Common questions

Do you prepare returns for people who don't own a business?

Yes. We prepare individual and family returns, federal and Missouri, starting at $250. You don't need to be a bookkeeping client.

What should I bring for my tax return?

Your W-2s and 1099s, any mortgage interest, property tax, tuition, or childcare statements, records of income and expenses if you're self-employed, and last year's return. We'll send a checklist based on your situation.

Can you file prior-year or late returns?

Yes. We prepare prior-year returns and can rebuild the books behind them if needed, all on one fixed proposal.

Do I need a CPA to do my taxes?

No. Tax returns can be prepared by paid preparers who aren't CPAs. We're an accounting and tax firm, not a CPA firm. If you need audit or attest services, we'll coordinate with a CPA.

When should I book for tax season?

As early as you can. We're booking now for the 2027 tax season, and earlier bookings get more flexible timing.
