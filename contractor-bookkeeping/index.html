Common questions

What is job costing for contractors?

Job costing assigns each expense, such as materials, labor, and subcontractors, to the specific job it belongs to. That lets you see the profit on each job instead of only the business as a whole, so you can spot underpriced work and bid more accurately.

When are 1099s due for subcontractors?

Form 1099-NEC for subcontractors you paid above the reporting threshold during the year ($2,000 for payments made in 2026 and later; $600 before that) is due to the IRS and to the contractor by January 31. Collecting a W-9 when you first hire a sub makes January far easier.

How should customer deposits be recorded?

A deposit for work you haven't done yet is a liability, not income. It's recognized as revenue as the work is completed. Booking deposits as income overstates profit and can make your tax picture look worse than it is.
