How catch-up works

Short answer: Catch-up bookkeeping brings a QuickBooks file current by reconciling every bank and card account month by month, fixing miscategorized transactions, and cleaning up the balance sheet. We quote it at about 0.75× your monthly rate for each month behind, and roughly half off when you start a monthly plan.

  1. Review. We look at your QuickBooks file (or bank statements, if there is no file) and your last tax return.
  2. Fixed quote. One price for the whole cleanup, in writing, before any work starts.
  3. Cleanup. Reconcile every account, fix categories, clear out duplicate and uncategorized transactions, and tie the balance sheet out to statements.
  4. Hand-off. You get current financials. From there we can keep you current monthly, prepare prior-year returns, or hand clean books to your CPA.

Typical cleanups take about a week once the scope is set and statements are in hand.

Common problems we fix

  • Bank feeds that were accepted without review, leaving duplicates and wrong categories
  • Hundreds of transactions sitting in "Uncategorized" or "Ask My Accountant"
  • Accounts that have never been reconciled, or reconciliations that don't match the statement
  • Loan payments booked entirely as expense instead of principal and interest
  • Owner personal spending mixed into business expenses
  • Undeposited Funds balances that never clear

Pricing example

If your monthly plan would be $400 and you're 6 months behind, catch-up is about 0.75 × $400 × 6 = $1,800. Start a monthly plan with us and it's roughly half that. Every quote is fixed before we start.

How long does catch-up bookkeeping take?

Yes. We can pair catch-up books with prior-year business and personal returns on one fixed proposal, so you get caught up on both.

What if I don't have QuickBooks set up at all?

That's fine. We can build the books from your bank and card statements in a new QuickBooks Online file.
