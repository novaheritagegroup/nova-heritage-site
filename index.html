Common questions

How do you keep my financial information secure?

We work in QuickBooks Online through accountant access, so you never share your login. Engagement letters are signed electronically, and we never ask for passwords or full account numbers by email. You can remove our access at any time.

Are you a CPA firm?

No. Nova Heritage Group is an accounting and tax firm, not a CPA firm. We handle bookkeeping, payroll, sales tax, and tax preparation and e-filing. We don't perform audits, reviews, or other attest services. If your bank or a contract requires a CPA-issued report, we'll get your books ready and coordinate with a CPA.

I already have a CPA for my taxes. Can you just do the books?

Yes. That's a common setup: you keep your CPA for the return and we handle the monthly work. We hand your CPA clean, reconciled books at year end.

How far behind is too far behind?

Not much is. Catch-up is a core service, and we can pair it with prior-year returns. You get one fixed quote for the whole cleanup before we start.

Do I have to be in the Kansas City area?

No. We're based in Lee's Summit and serve the whole Kansas City metro area on both sides of the state line, and everything also runs remotely through QuickBooks Online, email, and video calls, so clients anywhere in the U.S. are welcome. Each additional state return is quoted separately.

What software do you use?

QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Payroll for most clients, and Xero when you're already on it. Returns are prepared in professional tax software and filed electronically.

What's in the monthly dashboard?

A one-page view of revenue, net profit, cash, and who owes you money, plus margin by job type, client, or product line and a short note on what changed and what to watch. Launch plans get a simpler snapshot, and Department + Insights adds a live version with budget vs. actual and a 12-month forecast. See a sample.

How does the guarantee work?

If your books for a month aren't closed by the 10th of the next month, that month's bookkeeping fee is free. The clock starts once we have your bank and card access and any documents we asked for.

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans are month-to-month with 30 days' written notice to cancel. Tax preparation is a separate fixed-fee engagement for each tax year.
