Hi, I'm Greg

I founded Nova Heritage Group in Lee's Summit to give small businesses and families the kind of accounting help I'd want: someone who knows their numbers, answers their questions, and doesn't disappear between tax seasons.

I've worked as a full-charge bookkeeper, handling everything from daily entries and reconciliations through month-end close and financial statements. Alongside that I've built a career in data analytics, writing SQL and building reporting dashboards, which shapes how I approach your books: the numbers should tie out, and they should tell you something useful.

Credentials

  • QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor, Level 2
  • B.S. in Accounting
  • B.A. and M.A. in Economics
  • Full-charge bookkeeping experience

How we work

  • You work with me directly. No call center and no rotating staff. The person keeping your books is the person you talk to.
  • Fixed fees, published up front. Price ranges are on the website, and your exact fee is set in writing before we start.
  • Technology that saves you money, reviewed by a person. AI and automation speed up data entry and drafts; every deliverable and every return is checked by a person before it reaches you.
  • Honest about what we are. We're an accounting and tax firm, not a CPA firm. If you need an audit or other attest service, we'll get your books ready and bring in a CPA.

We work with small businesses of every kind, from contractors and consultants to retailers and landlords, and with individuals who need their return done right. Based in Lee's Summit, serving the Kansas City metro and clients anywhere in the U.S.
