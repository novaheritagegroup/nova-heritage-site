One firm for the books, payroll, and the return

Short answer: Nova Heritage Group keeps monthly QuickBooks Online books for small businesses throughout the Kansas City metro, in both Missouri and Kansas, for a fixed fee of $300–750 a month. We also run payroll, file sales tax, and prepare business and personal returns, so everything ties out.

Kansas City businesses deal with something most metros don't: two states. A contractor in Lee's Summit may have a job in Overland Park; a shop in Kansas City, Kansas may have employees who live in Missouri. That changes payroll withholding, sales tax, and which state returns get filed. We keep track of it as part of the monthly work, so it isn't a surprise in April.

What we handle

  • Monthly bookkeeping and reconciliations in QuickBooks Online (or Xero if you're already on it)
  • Payroll through QuickBooks Payroll, posted to the books every pay cycle
  • Missouri and Kansas sales tax filings for the states you sell in
  • 1099s for subcontractors, with W-9s collected during the year
  • Business returns (1120-S, 1065, 1120, Schedule C) and owner 1040s, federal and state, e-filed

Common questions

Both. We serve the whole Kansas City metro on both sides of the state line, including Overland Park, Olathe, Lenexa, and Kansas City, Kansas. Each additional state return is quoted separately.

Our fixed monthly fees run $300–750 depending on volume and complexity, with payroll (+$150–400/mo) and sales tax (+$75–150/mo) as add-ons. You get a fixed quote after we look at your books.

Can you handle payroll for employees in both Missouri and Kansas?

Yes. Payroll runs through QuickBooks Payroll, and we set up state withholding for where your employees live and work, then post every payroll to the books.
