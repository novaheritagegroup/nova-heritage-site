Text Message Consent

How Nova Heritage Group clients agree to receive text messages about their account, and how to stop them.

Effective November 23, 2026

Who receives these texts

Nova Heritage Group LLC sends text messages only to its business clients: owners who have signed a service agreement with us and given us a mobile number for their account. We do not text the general public, and we do not send marketing text messages.

How clients opt in

Clients opt in in writing when they sign our client service agreement. The agreement includes a text-message section where the client provides a mobile number and agrees to the statement below. Clients can choose not to receive texts and get the same notices by email instead.

Text message consent

Mobile number: ______________________

☐ I agree to receive text messages from Nova Heritage Group about my account, including new lead alerts, reply approval requests, missed-call notices and service updates. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of service. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

What we send

  • Alerts about new customer leads and missed calls for the client's business
  • Requests to approve or edit a suggested reply to a customer (for example, "Reply YES to send")
  • Notices that a lead needs the client's personal attention
  • Service updates about the client's account

Example: "Nova Heritage Group: New Thumbtack lead from Sarah (couch + mattress, Lee's Summit). Suggested reply: 'Hi Sarah! That's usually our minimum pickup, $125. Can you send a photo?' Reply YES to send, EDIT to change, or STOP to opt out."

First message

After a client opts in, the first text confirms enrollment: "Nova Heritage Group: You're set up to get lead alerts and reply approvals for your business. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel."

How to stop or get help

Reply STOP (or CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE) to any message to stop receiving texts. You'll get one confirmation and no further messages. Reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP for help, email greg@novaheritagegroupllc.com or call (913) 213-3503.

Privacy

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy for details.
