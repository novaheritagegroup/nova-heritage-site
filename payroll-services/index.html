What's included

Short answer: We run payroll through QuickBooks Payroll as an add-on to monthly bookkeeping, for $150–400 a month depending on headcount and pay frequency. That covers direct deposit, payroll tax deposits and filings through QuickBooks Payroll, year-end W-2s, and 1099s for contractors, with each payroll posted to your books.

  • Employee and owner setup, including S-corp owner salaries
  • Direct deposit on your pay schedule: weekly, every two weeks, twice a month, or monthly
  • Federal and state payroll tax deposits and quarterly filings through QuickBooks Payroll
  • W-2s for employees and 1099-NEC forms for contractors in January
  • Every payroll reconciled and posted to the books, so labor costs are right on your P&L

Why keep payroll with your bookkeeper

When payroll lives in a separate app, it's common to see payroll taxes double-counted, owner pay booked as an expense in the wrong place, or wage totals that don't match the W-2s. When the same firm runs payroll and keeps the books, it ties out every cycle and there's nothing to untangle at year end.

Common questions

How much do payroll services cost?

Payroll is $150–400 a month on top of a monthly bookkeeping plan, depending on the number of employees and how often you run payroll. QuickBooks Payroll subscription costs are quoted with your proposal.

Can you set up payroll for an S-corp owner?

Yes. S-corp owners who work in the business generally need to pay themselves a reasonable salary through payroll. We set that up and keep it coordinated with your distributions and tax return.

Can I switch payroll providers in the middle of the year?

Yes. We bring over year-to-date wages and tax payments so W-2s come out right in January.

Do you handle employees in both Missouri and Kansas?

Yes. We set up withholding for the states where your employees live and work.
